## Findings

1. **Critical** — I-6 incorrectly removes the HTLC CLTV floor entirely; stock HTLCs remove only the second success-CSV delay, not the Ark actualization prefix. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-conformance-matrix.md:269-271`; `docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:86-90,103-108`; `08-channels.md:317-340,1617-1658`) From force-close start, an HTLC claimant still crosses up to `D` sequential genesis confirmations, the bridge’s `d` CSV, bridge confirmation, commitment confirmation, and the stock claim. LDK was told the funding was already confirmed, so its ordinary buffers do not include `D+d`. A short final-hop/keysend CLTV can therefore expire before any claim is possible. This is distinct from the knowingly deferred success-vs-timeout race. Fix: define and enforce a stock profile floor such as `D + d + bridge/commitment/claim confirmation margin`; apply it to invoices, received HTLC validation, forwarding deltas, and per-HTLC force-close scheduling. Reject keysend unless the incoming HTLC itself meets that floor. Derive `slack` concretely and test boundary cases.

2. **Critical** — Trusted-0conf LDK cannot provide the specified registration-before-`ChannelReady` gate as planned. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:65-76,151,204-209,217-227`; `08-channels.md:543-574,622-646`) LDK 0.2.4 explicitly enables forwarding immediately after `accept_inbound_channel_from_trusted_peer_0conf` and emits `channel_ready` immediately (`rust-lightning@v0.2.4:lightning/src/ln/channelmanager.rs:9865-9893`; `lightning/src/ln/priv_short_conf_tests.rs:770-788`). The server could earn balance before registration arms its parent-exit defense. MR-0 also incorrectly proposes calling `unsafe_manual_funding_transaction_generated` “both sides”; only the funder receives `FundingGenerationReady` and calls it (`channelmanager.rs:6225-6253`). Fix: use ordinary inbound acceptance with nonzero minimum depth, call manual funding only on the client/funder, and inject the virtual funding confirmation only after complete Ark registration. MR-0 must assert absence of `SendChannelReady` and usability before that injection.

3. **Important** — The virtual-confirmation adapter has two unresolved stock-LDK incompatibilities: funding unconfirmation closes rather than suspends the channel, and virtual transaction positions can collide. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:137-141,217-247`; `08-channels.md:101-126`) LDK force-closes an active channel when its funding confirmation is withdrawn (`rust-lightning@v0.2.4:lightning/src/ln/channel.rs:11441-11470`), contradicting OP-21’s suspend-and-resume behavior. It also derives a real SCID from `(anchor height, supplied tx index, vout)` and asserts on collisions (`channel.rs:6370-6424`; `channelmanager.rs:3482-3488`); reusing an anchor’s actual transaction index for several virtual channels can panic. Fix: MR-0 must prove the chosen reorg gate and reconnection behavior, and define a deterministic, persistent, peer-consistent synthetic transaction-index allocation with collision handling. If resume cannot be achieved on stock LDK, document an explicit stricter profile or stop at the spike.

4. **Important** — EX-4 permits starting a downgrade at the force-close margin even though shutdown/HTLC drain/closing negotiation is unbounded. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-conformance-matrix.md:218,267`; `docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:100-102,281-286`; `08-channels.md:1209-1215,1245-1254,1642-1658`) Waiting for that downgrade can consume the remaining bridge-confirmation budget and lose the whole VTXO. Fix: schedule cooperative downgrade earlier under a separately derived lead; at the hard force-close margin, force-close unless complete split registration is already potentially final. Assign this scheduler and its peer-unresponsive/HTLC-drain tests to an MR.

5. **Important** — The unilateral-exit architecture omits LDK’s mandatory zero-fee `BumpTransaction` handling. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:134-142,276-290`; `08-channels.md:136-143,1573-1600`) In 0.2.4, commitment anchor bumps and HTLC claims arrive as `BumpTransactionEvent::{ChannelClose,HTLCResolution}`, requiring confirmed external inputs, persistent `claim_id` coin reservations, signing, package submission, and RBF handling (`rust-lightning@v0.2.4:lightning/src/events/bump_transaction/mod.rs:116-255`). A capture-only broadcaster plus bark’s `set_wallet_child_tx` is insufficient. Fix: make these events an explicit harness interface and extend MR-0 to prove force-close and HTLC resolution, not merely payment and cooperative close.

6. **Important** — I-9’s `resulting_depth ≤ pinned_max−2` does not guarantee a later cosignable split if the server lowers its live `max_vtxo_exit_depth`. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-conformance-matrix.md:274`; `docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:91-95`; `02-vtxo.md:28-36`; `08-channels.md:1371-1384`) The generic downgrade check uses the server’s then-current bound; the stored timing-profile maximum cannot override ARK #2. Fix: reject configuration decreases that would strand any live channel, or require those channels to downgrade first. Also require checked `M≥2`, retain the pre-close live-bound check, and test a max-depth decrease across restart.

7. **Important** — BR-17’s “fresh nonce per request” wording contradicts ARK #5’s duplicate-request nonce rule. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-conformance-matrix.md:67,107`; `05-arkoor.md:203-222,233-244`; `08-channels.md:1781-1787`) A byte-identical retransmission reuses the user public nonce; the server must replay its stored response or reject it, not create a new bridge signing session. Fix: say “fresh nonce for each fresh-nonce retry session,” make the bridge slot atomic with the transfer slots, and test fresh retry, identical duplicate, and identity-mismatch cases.

8. **Important** — Part 1 omits safety-relevant generic ARK #5 obligations used by both explicit channel entry points. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-conformance-matrix.md:70-109,137-176`; `05-arkoor.md:30-48,172-222,291-300,322-329`; `docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:120-127`) Missing checklist items include uniform checkpoint mode, base attestation verification, input existence/spendability/ban checks, rejection of an input already exiting in mempool or chain, balance/dust validation, atomic packages with distinct inputs, and full VTXO/anchor validation. Existing upstream machinery helps, but new explicit upgrade/downgrade paths could bypass it and no conformance test owns it. Fix: add inherited-baseline IDs and tests, especially “exiting at cosign” distinct from late registration.

9. **Important** — Cooperative-close capture is not specified as synchronously crash-durable at the instant LDK hands the transaction to the broadcaster. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:139-142,292-306`; `08-channels.md:1179-1202,1222-1227`) LDK calls `BroadcasterInterface` before producing the persisted `ChannelClosed` event, and that event does not contain the closing transaction (`rust-lightning@v0.2.4:lightning/src/ln/channelmanager.rs:12309-12323`; `lightning/src/events/mod.rs:1465-1510`). A crash after an in-memory capture loses the specifically required closing fallback. Fix: require the capture callback to durably commit before returning, then correlate it with the replayable close event; inject a crash between those operations.

10. **Important** — The advertised discharge accounting is incomplete and sometimes false. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:4,235-270,287-325`) BR-10, BR-11, BR-14 and DA-2..5 are claimed by no MR; I-1’s refresh-before-upgrade policy and I-2’s proactive downgrade scheduler are assigned to none; MR-1 cannot discharge BR-17’s runtime session behavior merely through construction helpers; and MR-3’s `OP-*` incorrectly includes explicitly parked OP-29. Fix: add a complete ID→owner→test/structural/pre-existing/profile-waiver table.

11. **Important** — The MR cuts omit checked-in artifacts that upstream CI requires and make MR-3/MR-5 excessively broad review units. (`docs/plans/2026-07-29-stage1-bark-upstreaming-plan.md:239-328`; `../bark@upstream/master:CONTRIBUTING.md:124-141`; `../bark@upstream/master:bark-rest/CLAUDE.md:42-52`) MR-2/MR-5 need `server/schema.sql`, `bark/schema.sql`, and `server/config.default.toml`; MR-6 must regenerate `bark-rest/openapi.json` and the entire `bark-rest-client`. Split MR-3 at least into protocol/server admission, registration/LDK activation, and client orchestration; similarly separate close capture/outcome from split/watch behavior.

12. **Minor** — Several matrix classifications need correction even though their cited ranges are accurate. BR-8 says the delay comes from live `vtxo_exit_delta` rather than the input’s decoded value (`matrix:58`; `08-channels.md:198-200,613-620`); EX-5 is marked fact despite a literal MUST (`matrix:219`; `08-channels.md:1642-1645`); RG-10 is marked literal MUST though the cited text is declarative (`matrix:127`; `08-channels.md:1454-1459`); and UE-4’s dependency on the excluded Ark channel type is missing from E-* (`matrix:208`; `08-channels.md:1598-1600`). Correct the wording/tags and add the entanglement.

## Verified clean

- Policy construction, bridge shape, upgrade ordering, amount/outpoint binding, downgrade balance rounding, dust isolation/660-sat floor, registration completeness, response watches, unilateral sequence, expiry sweep, and basic depth arithmetic matched their cited spec text.
- Removing exactly the second `pinned_exit_delta` contributed by the excluded success CSV is sound; the defect is removing the remaining per-HTLC Ark-prefix budget.
- I-1 does not require a server rule for money safety: any inherited conflict requires the same server to have double-signed, while the honest client bears the pre-existing arkoor trust. The client-policy resolution is sound, but currently unassigned.
- The §4.1 ldk-node rejection is correct: its public 0.7 surface exposes opinionated internal chain/wallet handling and no required raw LDK seams. ([0.7 Node API](https://docs.rs/ldk-node/latest/ldk_node/struct.Node.html), [builder source](https://docs.rs/ldk-node/latest/src/ldk_node/builder.rs.html))
- Upstream commit, migration numbers, free policy tag `0x08`, protobuf field availability, locked LDK 0.2.2 state, and `TransactionType` appearing only in 0.3 were verified against the requested refs.
